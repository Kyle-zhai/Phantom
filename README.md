## Why Phantom

Subscriptions are designed to be forgotten. Free trials roll into paid plans, prices creep up a dollar at a time, and the same kind of service ends up on the card twice. Most people don't notice until they sit down with a statement — and by then the money is gone.

Phantom does that sitting-down for you. Show it what you pay for, and in under a minute you see every recurring charge, what it costs you per year, and which ones are quietly wasting money.

**No bank login. No Phantom server. Your financial data never leaves your iPhone and your own iCloud.**

## What it does

### See everything you pay for
Import your Apple subscriptions, a screenshot of your banking app or card statement, or a CSV export. Phantom recognises a wide range of services — streaming and software, but also the ones people actually forget: dating apps, cloud storage, meal kits, security cameras, car washes, creator memberships and more.

### Know what to cut
Every subscription gets a **Zombie Score** from 0 to 100. Phantom flags services that overlap with something else you already pay for, services already included in a bundle or card perk you own, plans that cost more than a cheaper tier of the same service, and recent price hikes. Your own ratings shape the result, so the app learns what you actually value.

### "You already pay for this"
Tell Phantom which bundles, memberships and credit cards you have, and it points out subscriptions you're paying for twice — a streaming service already included in your phone plan, or a perk your card already reimburses.

### Keep it for less
When a subscription is worth keeping but overpriced, Phantom shows the cheaper plan, whether the service can be paused instead of cancelled, and like-for-like alternatives — free options first. **No affiliate links, ever.**

### Cancel with confidence
A step-by-step cancellation checklist opens the verified cancellation page for each service — web, phone or Apple — with no hunting through account menus. Save the confirmation number and a screenshot as proof, and Phantom reminds you to check your next statement to make sure the charges actually stopped.

### Negotiate a better price
Tried-and-tested retention scripts for dozens of popular services tell you what to say to get a discount or a free month before you cancel.

### Get your money back
If a service keeps charging after you cancelled, or a trial converted without warning, Phantom drafts a formal dispute letter that cites your rights under U.S. consumer-protection law. If the merchant ignores it, Phantom prepares a chargeback request for your card issuer, with the right language and the official complaint channels.

### Stay ahead of the next charge
Local notifications warn you before a free trial converts, when a price goes up and when it's time to review your subscriptions again. A home-screen and lock-screen widget keeps your monthly spend in view.

### Share the win
Generate a clean savings card showing what you found or what you saved, ready to post or send.

## Privacy, by design

Phantom was built on three promises:

- **We never sell your data.**
- **We never push loans or financial products.**
- **We never see your bank login or card number.**

Screenshots are read on your device. There is no Phantom backend, no analytics SDK and no third-party tracking. Optional iCloud sync stores your data in your own Apple account, and deleting your account and data is a single tap in Settings. Full details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://kyle-zhai.github.io/Phantom/privacy.html).

## Plans

Phantom is free to download. The free plan finds your subscriptions, scores them, surfaces your single biggest saving and includes a dispute letter every month. **Phantom Pro** removes the limits: every subscription and finding, the full score breakdown, price-hike alerts, negotiation scripts, cheaper plans and alternatives, and unlimited dispute letters.

## Requirements

iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Dispute letters and chargeback guidance are written for U.S. consumer-protection law.
